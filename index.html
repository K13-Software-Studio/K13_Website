Four levels of craft, chosen part by part

Not every part of a site deserves the same effort. You see each level on your own site before you choose, and I will tell you where not to spend.

  1. EssentialIt works, it is clean, nothing is missing. Most sites should be exactly this.
  2. SignatureIt carries your character instead of looking like anyone's.
  3. TailoredCut for you alone. It only exists on your site.
  4. ImmersiveThe part people remember and show someone else. Two or three per site, at most.
