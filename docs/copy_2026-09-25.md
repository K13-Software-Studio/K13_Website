## Summary for James

**Headline chosen:** "Software that feels obvious, built by two people who *answer*." Kept the
existing "feels obvious" signature line (it already carries the brand and appears again in the
capabilities section) instead of inventing a new hook, but retired every "I'm Kazim, solo
builder" line under it. The new payoff word is "answer": it's honest (two named people really do
reply), it's the throughline for the "who it's for" doors ("a partner who answers"), and it
needed only one italic word to land, matching the page's existing one-italic-word heading rule.

**Couldn't write without a decision:**
1. The exact verification date for "On the record" and the work-section foot ("All 13 checked
   live on [DATE]") · left as a placeholder; fill at ship time, not before, since re-verifying
   earlier makes the date stale by the actual ship date.
2. TrustMeBro workbench game uses invented example odds (+165/−160, 42%/55%) as clearly
   illustrative demo content, not a business claim; flagging since the house rule against
   invented numbers is otherwise absolute, but a teaching toy needs numbers to teach with, the
   same way Carlos's brush is explicitly labelled "a toy, not his work."
3. Two small existing-code fixes surfaced while matching current copy against the hard rules,
   not new ones: the contact fallback line uses "me" and a literal em dash (`&mdash;`), and
   Step 3's question still reads "do I reach you." Both are one-line swaps, included above.
